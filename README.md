# 📊 AI/ML Compensation & Workforce Evolution (2020–2025)

An executive-level data analytics and visualization project examining **71,913 records** of global AI, ML, and Data compensation trends. This project translates raw workforce data into strategic insights on salary compression, Return-to-Office (RTO) shifts, and geographic pay arbitrage.

---

## Executive Summary (BLUF)

Between 2020 and 2025, the AI and Data labor market underwent a major structural transformation. Key findings include:

* **The AI Salary Premium:** AI Architects and specialized ML Engineers command top-tier compensation bands ($194k–$215k median), while traditional Data Analyst positions experience salary stagnation.
* **The RTO Pivot:** Remote work peaked at **55.7%** in 2022 before collapsing. By 2025, **76.5%** of roles stabilized as mandatory On-site, signaling an industry-wide return to binary work policies (strictly on-site or strictly remote).
* **Geographic Arbitrage:** US median salaries ($185,000) command a **2.8x pay differential** over secondary international tech hubs (e.g., New Zealand at $65,000), opening significant opportunities for global talent optimization.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Talent Allocation & Upskilling:** Shift acquisition budgets toward specialized AI/ML Engineers. Transition existing Data Analysts into **Analytics Engineers** or **AI Integration Specialists** (via LLM orchestration, dbt, and automated pipelines).
2. **Incentive Structuring:** Cap cash base salaries for executive roles near Senior market thresholds, shifting high-end upside into **performance-contingent equity** tied to platform deployment milestones.
3. **Localized Rewards:** Avoid uniform global USD salary scales. Anchor international offers to the top 10–15% of the candidate's local country market to scale headcount while protecting gross margins.
4. **Binary Work Policies:** Eliminate vague "2-day hybrid" policies. Reserve a targeted **20–25% remote allocation** strictly as a talent acquisition tool for hard-to-fill, principal-level technical roles.
